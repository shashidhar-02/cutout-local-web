# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in the Cutout community, regardless of background, identity, or technical experience.

## Standards & Expectations

Positive behaviors include:
- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and respect in all discussions
- Providing constructive feedback on pull requests and issues
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community and project users
- Protecting user privacy and upholding ethical computing practices

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Harassment, derogatory commentary, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information or sensitive photos without consent
- Discriminatory language or exclusionary conduct

## Reporting & Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for upholding these community standards and addressing any behavior that compromises a safe and respectful environment. Inquiries and reports can be submitted through GitHub repository issues or maintainer contact channels.
